# B22 - Audio sample multiplication and echoing in multiplayer

## Symptoms

- In games with more than 2 players, audio begins to echo or multiply. It sounds like the same effect is being triggered
  several times offset by a frame or two.
- This causes significant FPS drops and audio clutter over the session.

## Investigation

- It is unclear if there's a replication loop, or if multiple clients are mistakenly broadcasting the same audio event.
- Check `unaudiospatial-plugin` and `unreplicon-plugin` to trace how `PlayPositionalSoundBroadcast` or similar events
  are replicated.
- Ensure that the authority is the only one generating the sound event and that clients are securely receiving exactly
  one instance.
- Check if old sound entities are failing to despawn in multiplayer, causing entity pileups.
