## Symptoms

- Walkie-lady (unwalkie) doesn't play feedback for wrong guess of repellent or wrong ghost.
- Should always play when the decision was wrong.

## Investigation

- Check `repellent_feedback.rs` and other evidence gathering logic.

## Testing

Tested several times, under different difficulties, the voice line never appears in multiplayer. However it does appear

## Testing 2

This was partially fixed. The problem now is that voice lines can be cancelled by the server.

In the old method - for local only, the systems were constantly trying to play. On the new method for multiplayer, they
try once, they register as triggered locally and they stop. This makes important messages such as lack of evidence to be
often missed.
