# B01 - Ghost roars cannot be heard

## Symptoms

- Ghost roars are not heard by "regular" players who join during the initial mission load.
- It works for late joiners or after rejoining.
- Potentially related to `assets/sounds/ghost-roar-1.ogg`.

## Investigation

- Need to find where `ghost-roar-1.ogg` is used.
- Check synchronization of `GhostSoundFieldBroadcast`.
- Investigate difference between auto-join and manual join.

## Findings

- Ghost roars are handled via `GhostVocalization` component changes.
- `GhostVocalization` is replicated by Replicon.
- `play_vocalization_sounds` in `unghost-presentation/src/systems/interaction_audio.rs` listens for changes and plays
  the sound locally.
- It includes a 5-second staleness check.
- Action: Added diagnostic logging to `play_vocalization_sounds` to track when vocalizations are played or skipped due
  to staleness. This will help diagnose if the first client is missing the initial "change" event or if the roar is
  arriving before the client is ready to play audio.

## Testing

Seems fixed for now.
