# B19 - Multiplayer difficulty tuning (players not dying)

## Symptoms

- Even on "Hard" difficulty and across various maps, a group of new players managed to survive prolonged sessions
  without anyone dying.
- The game currently feels too forgiving; the ghost is not dealing enough lethal damage to pose a real threat in
  multiplayer.

## Investigation

- This is a difficulty tuning task, not a broken logic bug. The damage system correctly works, but the tuning may be too
  benevolent.
- Review `unghost` and `unvitals` for multiplayer damage scaling.
- Check if the recent fix that prevented the ghost from killing a second person within 0.1s of the first kill
  inadvertently nerfed overall damage or aggression.
- Adjust damage numbers, difficulty multipliers, and aggression.
