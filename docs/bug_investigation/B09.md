# B09 - Truck interaction sounds should be shared

## Symptoms
- "End Mission" and "Craft Repellent" sounds are only heard by the player who clicks.

## Investigation
- Make these sounds positional and audible to all players near the truck.
- Use a broadcast message or spawn a replicated sound entity.

## Findings
- Added `PlayPositionalSoundBroadcast` message to `unreplicon-core`.
- Handled `PlayPositionalSoundBroadcast` in `unreplicon-plugin` to play positional audio on all clients.
- Updated `truckui_event_handle` in `untruck-plugin` to broadcast the "dingdingding" sound when "End Mission" or "Craft Repellent" is clicked on the authority.
- Sounds are now localized at the `VanEntryPoint`.
