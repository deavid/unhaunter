# B02 - Salt particles are not despawned

## Symptoms
- `SaltyTrace` particles persist between missions.

## Investigation
- Check `SaltyTrace` component and its spawning logic.
- Find where mission cleanup happens and ensure these are included.

## Findings
- `SaltyTrace` entities are spawned by the ghost movement system with `Replicated`.
- They have a `SaltyTraceTimer` (600s) but don't seem to have `GameSprite` on the server/authority if they are just data-only entities there.
- Actually, `hydrate_salty_trace_visuals` adds `GameSprite` on clients/host.
- On the server, they might not have `GameSprite`.
- Action: Ensure `SaltyTrace` entities are cleaned up during mission teardown on the server.
